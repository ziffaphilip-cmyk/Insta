## Topic and angle
The pressure to have your twenties figured out comes from picturing the decade as a highlight reel.
Lived from the inside, it's mostly admin and small decisions that only make sense later. Funny first,
then a real point, without turning it into a lesson.

## Who it's for
Women in their twenties who feel behind: on career, relationships or "the plan".

## Why it's worth posting
A life topic that stands on its own, no fitness link forced. The humour makes it shareable in group
chats, and the honest ending builds connection. The gym only appears in passing, which is the point:
it's part of your life, not the whole of it.

## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
> Nobody told me your twenties are mostly admin.

Visual: you at a laptop, deadpan, mid-email.

## Your input needed
- Your age for the bracket (or say "if you're in your twenties" instead).
- Optional: swap any admin example for one that's genuinely yours. Specific is funnier.

## Script
```
Nobody told me your twenties are mostly admin.

I thought this decade would be big decisions.
Career. City. Who I'm going to be.
And those are in there, somewhere.

But mostly it's booking appointments.
Doing your tax return.
Remembering to buy bin bags.
Replying to the email you've been avoiding for a week.

And somewhere in between all that,
you're meant to have a five-year plan.

I think the pressure comes from looking at the decade from the outside,
like it's one big highlight reel.
From the inside it's a lot of small, boring decisions
that only make sense later.

So if you're [26] and you don't know exactly where it's going:
same.
I know I've got the gym at six,
and the bins go out on Thursday.

That's the plan.
```

## B-roll and visuals
