Apricot Hour

A calm, warm, text-led style for lifestyle reels. It keeps what works in your three references: the story told in on-screen text, fast cuts under a still title, and music doing the emotional work. The fonts, colours, motion and signature details are new and yours.

Every frame on this page is a real render made with the brand's fonts and colour grade, shown over stills from your reference clips for illustration only. Your own footage replaces them.

01 · What the references share

What I learned, and what changes

All three clips are 8 to 10 seconds, vertical, with no voiceover. The table separates the habits worth keeping from the look, which I've replaced so your reels don't read as copies.

03 · Colour

Warm text, one accent

Apricot appears once per text block: one title word, one caption word, or the time. Text never sits on a coloured box. A soft espresso shadow keeps it readable on bright walls and skies.

04 · Motion

The signature reveal

  • Title wordsEach word rises 24 px from a 10 px blur to sharp over 7 frames, with 4 frames between words. Ease-out.
  • UnderlineA hand-drawn apricot stroke draws under the accent word over 10 frames, starting as the last word lands.
  • Title holdThe title stays still on screen for 3–4 seconds while 3–5 clips cut underneath, then fades out over 6 frames on a beat.
  • CaptionsHard swap on the beat with a 3-frame fade. No bouncing, scaling or typewriter effects.
  • Chapter markerFades in over 5 frames on the cut that starts the chapter. Stays for the whole chapter.
  • CameraSlow 100→104% push on holds longer than 1.5s. No shake, spins or glitch effects.
  • TransitionsStraight cuts. A 4-frame dip through a warm flash is allowed once per reel, at the chapter change.

06 · On screen

Where things go

Text stays inside fixed bands that clear Instagram's buttons, username and caption. If the title band would cover a face, the title moves up into the 18–35% band instead.

07 · Grade, sound and export

The finishing rules

  • GradeContrast −4%, saturation +6%, warmer mids and highlights, blacks lifted to 3.5%, whites capped at 97.5%. Exact FFmpeg settings are saved in your brand file.
  • MusicOne track per reel: mellow R&B, lo-fi or soft pop instrumental, 85–110 BPM. Cuts land on the beat. Trending sounds get added in the Instagram app so they stay licensed.
  • LoudnessMaster at −14 LUFS with peaks under −1 dB. If you speak on camera, music drops 12 dB under your voice.
  • Sound effectsJust one: a soft paper tick at −28 dB when the title underline draws. Nothing else.
  • Length8–15 seconds by default. Up to 30 seconds for tutorials or splits.
  • Export1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 High at 16 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps. A clean copy without text is saved too.
  • CheckBefore I deliver: text inside the safe zones, one accent per block, nothing on screen under 0.5s, the first frame works as the cover, the ending loops.
